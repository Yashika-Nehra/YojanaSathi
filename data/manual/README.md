# YojanaSathi curated catalogue

This file contains a small, manually reviewed starter catalogue for the hackathon. It is not a claim of containing every government scheme in India.

Primary catalogue sources checked on 20 September 2026 include:

- National Scholarship Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships
- National Scholarship Portal student application area: https://scholarships.gov.in/Students
- National Government Services Portal: https://services.india.gov.in/
- National Internship Portal by AICTE: https://internship.aicte-india.org/
- National Career Service: https://ncs.gov.in/
- myScheme: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/
- Official PM and ministry portals linked from individual records.

## Adding more records

Each record should preserve the normalized fields used by the backend. Do not invent URLs or deadlines. When an exact value is not known, use `null` and add an `eligibility_note` explaining what still needs verification.

For a full catalogue, use an authorized government dataset/API or a manually reviewed export. Do not silently scrape or mirror a source if its terms prohibit redistribution.
